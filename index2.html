About The World Cup

The FIFA World Cup™ is the pinnacle of international football. Held every four years, it unites the finest national squads across continents to battle for the most coveted trophy in sports.

Since Uruguay hosted the inaugural tournament in 1930, the World Cup has grown into a global cultural spectacle. Every edition delivers timeless drama—from breathtaking bicycle kicks and nerve-wracking penalty shootouts to heroic underdog triumphs that defy the odds.

Beyond the pitch, it serves as a beacon of unity, bringing billions of fans together across languages, borders, and generations under a single universal passion.

UNFORGETTABLE DRAMA

Unexpected & Shocking Moments

From monumental upsets to unbelievable emotional outbursts, the World Cup writes stories no scriptwriter could invent.

1950 THE MARACANAZO

Uruguay Stuns 200,000 Brazilians

In what remains the most shocking defeat in football history, tournament hosts Brazil only needed a draw to claim their first title. In front of an estimated 200,000 spectators at the Maracanã, Uruguay came from behind to win 2–1, plunging an entire nation into national mourning.

1990 TEARS IN TURIN

Gazza’s Iconic Heartbreak

England midfielder Paul Gascoigne captured the hearts of millions during the 1990 semi-final against West Germany. Upon receiving a booking that would have banned him from the final, tears rolled down his face—becoming one of television's most poignant sports images.

1994 TRAGIC TWIST

The Andrés Escobar Own Goal

Colombia entered the 1994 World Cup as dark-horse favorites. However, an accidental own goal by defender Andrés Escobar led to a 2–1 defeat against the USA, resulting in their early elimination in one of the tournament's most sorrowful chapters.

2002 CHAMPIONS CRASH

Reigning Champions France Fail to Score

Defending champions France, boasting the top goal-scorers from England, Italy, and France, were stunned 1–0 by debutants Senegal in the opening match and crashed out in the group stage without scoring a single goal throughout the entire competition.

2022 LUSAIL MIRACLE

Saudi Arabia Shocks Champion Argentina

In one of the greatest opening-round upsets in history, Saudi Arabia defeated eventual champions Argentina 2–1 with two sublime second-half goals, snapping Argentina's 36-match unbeaten streak and setting the stage for Messi’s miraculous redemption run.

IMMORTALS

World Cup Legends & Records

The iconic players whose names are etched into football eternity.

⭐⭐⭐
1958, 1962, 1970 🇧🇷 Brazil

Pelé (Edson Arantes do Nascimento)

🏆 Record: Only 3-Time World Cup Winner

Pelé burst onto the global stage in 1958 as a fearless 17-year-old, netting two breathtaking goals in the final. Blessed with unparalleled vision, supreme athleticism, and lethal precision, "O Rei" transformed football into Joga Bonito and won three World Cups across 12 unforgettable years.

⭐
1986 🇦🇷 Argentina

Diego Armando Maradona

⚡ Masterpiece: The "Goal of the Century"

Maradona delivered the single most dominant individual tournament performance in football history at Mexico 1986. Against England, he scored both the infamous "Hand of God" and slalomed past five defenders for the immortal "Goal of the Century," single-handedly lifting Argentina to the crown.

⭐
2022 🇦🇷 Argentina

Lionel Messi

👑 Fairytale Completed: Golden Ball Winner

Lionel Messi achieved sporting immortality at Qatar 2022. Captaining Argentina through an electrifying campaign, he recorded 7 goals and 3 assists, including a heroic brace in the greatest final ever played against France, completing football's most celebrated career resume.

⭐ 5 Tournaments
2006 – 2022 🇵🇹 Portugal

Cristiano Ronaldo

🎯 Record: Scored in 5 Separate World Cups

A testament to unmatched dedication, discipline, and longevity. Across five tournaments spanning 16 years, Cristiano Ronaldo became the first and only male footballer in history to score in five consecutive World Cup editions, forever cementing his place in tournament lore.
